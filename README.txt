MICHAEL JACKSON — ONE-PAGE SCROLL WEBSITE

This version combines the original Home Page (Page 1) and the Albums page (Page 2) into ONE continuous page.

How it works:
- Open index.html (GitHub Pages will do this automatically).
- The original Home Page appears first, with its original design and content.
- Scroll down past the biography to reach ALBUMS OF MJ (Off the Wall, Thriller, Bad).
- There is no hamburger menu and no separate albums page.

Upload all files in this ZIP directly into the ROOT of your existing Michael-jackson repository, replacing the old files with the same names. Do not upload the containing folder itself.

Files: index.html, style.css, script.js, albums.css, albums.js, image-credits.txt.
Album artwork loads from Cover Art Archive; the portrait loads from Wikimedia Commons, so those images need an internet connection.


MJ RADIO UPDATE
- Added a dedicated MJ RADIO section to index.html.
- The live player is provided by MjTunes at https://www.mjtunes.com/?clang=en.
- Press Play inside the embedded player. If your browser blocks the embedded player, use the OPEN MJ RADIO LIVE link.
- The radio service runs its own stream; this website does not host, copy, or rebroadcast music.
- The MjTunes catalogue includes Michael Jackson solo music and related Jackson 5 / The Jacksons recordings.
- Internet access is required. Playback may require a tap and may open in a separate player window.


MJ RADIO: A dedicated radio section appears immediately after the homepage hero. It embeds the MjTunes player and provides an external link if embedding is unavailable. The radio is supplied by MjTunes; this site does not host audio.


MJ RADIO — LARGE YOUTUBE PLAYER UPDATE
- The MJ RADIO section now contains a large, responsive 16:9 YouTube playlist player.
- PLAY ALL starts the embedded playlist after the visitor taps, respecting browser autoplay-with-sound restrictions.
- OPEN PLAYLIST ON YOUTUBE opens the playlist directly on YouTube.
- The player is responsive on phones and desktop; it is no longer a narrow/tiny embedded panel.
- Playlist contents and order remain controlled by YouTube and the playlist owner; this ZIP does not claim to create or reorder the playlist.


REAL MUSIC CATALOGUE UPDATE
- Real Music now has separate Solo Studio Albums, Remix Albums, and Posthumous Albums categories.
- Added dedicated album pages and standard-edition tracklists for Music & Me, Forever, Michael, Off the Wall, Thriller, Bad, Dangerous, HIStory, Invincible, Blood on the Dance Floor: HIStory in the Mix, Michael (2010), and Xscape.
- Blood on the Dance Floor remains correctly classified as a remix album released during Michael Jackson's lifetime.
- Michael (2010) and Xscape (2014) are listed as posthumous albums.
- New pages do not contain guessed live-performance links. Their performance sections clearly state that verified event/video matches have not yet been added. Existing Got to Be There and Ben pages are preserved.
- Jackson 5 and The Jacksons group albums have not been added to these categories.


LIVE PERFORMANCE ARCHIVE UPDATE
A new live-performances.html page links to show-by-show records and lists documented examples for songs with reliable archive entries. It deliberately does NOT invent ten appearances for songs without ten documented performances, and it does not claim every song has a surviving video. Archive pages may include audio-only and setlist-only evidence.
