WHO IS MJ?

Michael Jackson (August 29, 1958 – June 25, 2009), often called MJ or the "King of Pop," was an American singer, songwriter, dancer, and philanthropist who reshaped pop music and visual performance.

CAREER & LEGACY

EARLY START

Rose to fame as the lead singer of The Jackson 5 in the late 1960s before launching a landmark solo career.

ICONIC ALBUMS

Released chart-topping and influential records including Off the Wall (1979), Thriller (1982)—the best-selling album of all time—Bad (1987), Dangerous (1991), and HIStory (1995).

DANCE & STAGE CRAFT

Popularized signature moves like the moonwalk, the robot, and the anti-gravity lean, turning music videos into cinematic shorts.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Sold over 500 million records worldwide as a solo artist, earned 13 Grammy Awards, and scored 13 Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles.

PERSONAL LIFE & LATER YEARS

Born in Gary, Indiana, and died at age 50 in Los Angeles from acute propofol intoxication. His life and music have been explored across documentaries and films, including Michael Jackson's This Is It (2009), Leaving Neverland (2019), and the 2026 biopic Michael.

THE MUSIC • THE MASTERPIECES

ALBUMS OF MJ

Three landmark chapters in the musical journey of the King of Pop.

01 / 1979

OFF THE WALL

OVERVIEW
Released on August 10, 1979, via Epic Records and produced by Quincy Jones. It was Jackson's fifth studio album and his breakthrough as an adult solo artist.

SOUND & THEMES
Blends disco, pop, funk, R&B, and soft rock, exploring themes of escapism, freedom, romance, and self-expression.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features hit singles including "Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough," "Rock with You," "She's Out of My Life," and "Off the Wall." Jackson became the first solo artist to have four singles from one album reach the Billboard Hot 100 top 10, with worldwide sales exceeding 30 million copies.

02 / 1982

THRILLER

OVERVIEW
Released on November 29, 1982, via Epic Records, Thriller was Jackson's sixth studio album and his second collaboration with producer Quincy Jones.

SOUND & THEMES
Moving away from the pure disco of Off the Wall, it blends post-disco, pop, funk, rock, and R&B, exploring themes of paranoia, romance, and the supernatural.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Billie Jean," "Beat It" (with Eddie Van Halen), "Thriller," "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'," and "Human Nature." Seven of its nine tracks reached the Billboard Hot 100 top 10. Driven by groundbreaking MTV music videos and Jackson's debut of the moonwalk, it won a record eight Grammy Awards and sold over 70 million copies worldwide.

03 / 1987

BAD

OVERVIEW
Released on August 31, 1987, Bad was Jackson's seventh studio album and his third and final project with Quincy Jones. Jackson took far greater creative control, writing and co-producing 9 of the 11 tracks.

SOUND & THEMES
Uses sharp digital synthesizers and heavy drum programming for a harder, more aggressive dance-pop, rock, and R&B sound. Lyrics tackle media scrutiny, street toughness, romance, and global unity.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Bad," "The Way You Make Me Feel," "Man in the Mirror," "Dirty Diana," "I Just Can't Stop Loving You," and "Smooth Criminal." It became the first album in history to produce five consecutive Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 singles and sold over 35 million copies worldwide.
