WHO IS MJ?

Michael Jackson (August 29, 1958 – June 25, 2009), often called MJ or the "King of Pop," was an American singer, songwriter, dancer, and philanthropist who reshaped pop music and visual performance.

CAREER & LEGACY

EARLY START

Rose to fame as the lead singer of The Jackson 5 in the late 1960s before launching a landmark solo career.

ICONIC ALBUMS

Released chart-topping and influential records including Off the Wall (1979), Thriller (1982)—the best-selling album of all time—Bad (1987), Dangerous (1991), and HIStory (1995).

DANCE & STAGE CRAFT

Popularized signature moves like the moonwalk, the robot, and the anti-gravity lean, turning music videos into cinematic shorts.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Sold over 500 million records worldwide as a solo artist, earned 13 Grammy Awards, and scored 13 Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles.

PERSONAL LIFE & LATER YEARS

Born in Gary, Indiana, and died at age 50 in Los Angeles from acute propofol intoxication. His life and music have been explored across documentaries and films, including Michael Jackson's This Is It (2009), Leaving Neverland (2019), and the 2026 biopic Michael.
