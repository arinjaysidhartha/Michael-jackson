WHO IS MJ?

Michael Jackson (August 29, 1958 – June 25, 2009), often called MJ or the "King of Pop," was an American singer, songwriter, dancer, and philanthropist who reshaped pop music and visual performance.

CAREER & LEGACY

EARLY START

Rose to fame as the lead singer of The Jackson 5 in the late 1960s before launching a landmark solo career.

ICONIC ALBUMS

Released chart-topping and influential records including Off the Wall (1979), Thriller (1982)—the best-selling album of all time—Bad (1987), Dangerous (1991), and HIStory (1995).

DANCE & STAGE CRAFT

Popularized signature moves like the moonwalk, the robot, and the anti-gravity lean, turning music videos into cinematic shorts.

ACHIEVEMENTS

Sold over 500 million records worldwide as a solo artist, earned 13 Grammy Awards, and scored 13 Billboard Hot 100 number-one singles.

PERSONAL LIFE & LATER YEARS

Born in Gary, Indiana, and died at age 50 in Los Angeles from acute propofol intoxication. His life and music have been explored across documentaries and films, including Michael Jackson's This Is It (2009), Leaving Neverland (2019), and the 2026 biopic Michael.

THE MUSIC • THE MASTERPIECES

ALBUMS OF MJ

Explore landmark chapters in the musical journey of the King of Pop.

01 / 1972

GOT TO BE THERE

OVERVIEW
Released on January 24, 1972, via Motown, Got to Be There was Michael Jackson's debut solo studio album, executive-produced by Berry Gordy while Jackson was still performing with the Jackson 5.

SOUND & THEMES
Blending classic Motown soul orchestration and soft rock with 1950s rock-and-roll throwbacks, it captures his transitioning boy-soprano voice across themes of youthful devotion, friendship, and heartbreak.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Got to Be There," "Rockin' Robin," "I Wanna Be Where You Are," "Ain't No Sunshine," and "You've Got a Friend." Three singles reached the Billboard Hot 100 top 20, proving the 13-year-old could sustain a solo career independently, breaking him out of the teen-pop mold with mature covers, and selling over 3.2 million copies worldwide.

02 / 1972

BEN

OVERVIEW
Released on August 4, 1972, via Motown, Ben was Michael Jackson's second solo studio album, released just seven months after his debut while he was still fronting the Jackson 5.

SOUND & THEMES
Continuing his classic Motown soul and pop sound with lush orchestration, it pairs vulnerable adult balladeering with upbeat R&B covers across themes of loyalty, friendship, love, and social awareness.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Ben," "People Make the World Go 'Round," "My Girl," "Everybody's Somebody's Fool," and "We've Got a Good Thing Going". Peaking at No. 5 on the Billboard 200, it produced his first solo Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 single ("Ben"), earned a Golden Globe win and an Oscar nomination, and sold over 5 million copies worldwide.

03 / 1973

MUSIC & ME

OVERVIEW
Released on April 13, 1973, via Motown, Music & Me was Michael Jackson's third solo studio album, recorded at age 14 while touring globally with the Jackson 5.

SOUND & THEMES
Featuring mellow pop, soul, and string-heavy ballads, it captures his voice shifting through adolescence across themes of childhood innocence, love, longing, and his personal bond with music.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "With a Child's Heart," "Music and Me," "Happy," "Morning Glow," and "Too Young". Peaking at No. 92 on the Billboard 200 with minimal promotion, it remains his lowest-selling solo album at around 2 million copies worldwide, while Motown's refusal to let him write or play on the record fueled his push for creative independence

04 / 1975

FOREVER, MICHAEL

OVERVIEW
Released on January 16, 1975, via Motown, Forever, Michael was Michael Jackson's fourth solo studio album and his final solo release under the label before departing for Epic Records.

SOUND & THEMES
Adopting a smoother Philadelphia soul and funk sound, it marked his transition from a boy soprano to a tenor while exploring mature themes of romance, devotion, and growing up.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "We're Almost There," "Just a Little Bit of You," "One Day in Your Life," "Take Me Back," and "Dear Michael". Peaking at No. 101 on the Billboard 200 and selling over 1 million copies worldwide, it closed his Motown solo era and later produced his first UK No. 1 solo single when "One Day in Your Life" was re-released in 1981.

05 / 1979

OFF THE WALL

OVERVIEW
Released on August 10, 1979, via Epic Records and produced by Quincy Jones. It was Jackson's fifth studio album and his breakthrough as an adult solo artist.

SOUND & THEMES
Blends disco, pop, funk, R&B, and soft rock, exploring themes of escapism, freedom, romance, and self-expression.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features hit singles including "Don't Stop 'Til You Get Enough," "Rock with You," "She's Out of My Life," and "Off the Wall." Jackson became the first solo artist to have four singles from one album reach the Billboard Hot 100 top 10, with worldwide sales exceeding 30 million copies.

06 / 1982

THRILLER

OVERVIEW
Released on November 29, 1982, via Epic Records, Thriller was Jackson's sixth studio album and his second collaboration with producer Quincy Jones.

SOUND & THEMES
Moving away from the pure disco of Off the Wall, it blends post-disco, pop, funk, rock, and R&B, exploring themes of paranoia, romance, and the supernatural.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Billie Jean," "Beat It" (with Eddie Van Halen), "Thriller," "Wanna Be Startin' Somethin'," and "Human Nature." Seven of its nine tracks reached the Billboard Hot 100 top 10. Driven by groundbreaking MTV music videos and Jackson's debut of the moonwalk, it won a record eight Grammy Awards and sold over 70 million copies worldwide.

07 / 1987

BAD

OVERVIEW
Released on August 31, 1987, Bad was Jackson's seventh studio album and his third and final project with Quincy Jones. Jackson took far greater creative control, writing and co-producing 9 of the 11 tracks.

SOUND & THEMES
Uses sharp digital synthesizers and heavy drum programming for a harder, more aggressive dance-pop, rock, and R&B sound. Lyrics tackle media scrutiny, street toughness, romance, and global unity.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Bad," "The Way You Make Me Feel," "Man in the Mirror," "Dirty Diana," "I Just Can't Stop Loving You," and "Smooth Criminal." It became the first album in history to produce five consecutive Billboard Hot 100 No. 1 singles and sold over 35 million copies worldwide.

08 / 1991

DANGEROUS

OVERVIEW
Released on November 21, 1991, via Epic Records, Dangerous was Michael Jackson's eighth studio album and his first since 1975 without producer Quincy Jones, instead pairing him with Teddy Riley and Bill Bottrell.

SOUND & THEMES
Pioneering a sharp new jack swing sound blended with industrial funk, R&B, gospel, and rock, it explores darker themes of lust, social injustice, racial harmony, and personal vulnerability.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Black or White," "Remember the Time," "In the Closet," "Jam," and "Heal the World". Debuting at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, it produced four US top-10 singles, popularized new jack swing globally, sparked the landmark Super Bowl XXVII halftime show, and sold over 32 million copies worldwide.

09 / 1995

HISTORY: PAST, PRESENT AND FUTURE, BOOK I

OVERVIEW
Released on June 20, 1995, via Epic and MJJ Productions, HIStory was a two-disc set combining a 15-track greatest hits disc with Jackson's ninth studio album of 15 new songs.

SOUND & THEMES
Fusing hard-hitting hip-hop beats, orchestral pop, R&B, and rock, it is his most autobiographical record, tackling media scrutiny, isolation, greed, injustice, and environmental awareness.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Scream" (with Janet Jackson), "You Are Not Alone," "Earth Song," "They Don't Care About Us," and "Stranger in Moscow". It debuted at No. 1 in 20 countries, produced the first single to debut at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 ("You Are Not Alone"), launched his highest-grossing solo tour, and became the best-selling multi-disc album of all time.

10 / 2001

INVINCIBLE

OVERVIEW
Released on October 30, 2001, via Epic Records, Invincible was Michael Jackson's tenth and final studio album of original material released during his lifetime, primarily produced with Rodney Jerkins.

SOUND & THEMES
Adopting a sleek, bass-heavy 2000s R&B, neo-soul, and futuristic pop sound, it balances smooth romantic ballads with sharp critiques of media intrusion, betrayal, and isolation.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "You Rock My World," "Butterflies," "Cry," "Unbreakable," and "Break of Dawn". Despite debuting at No. 1 in 14 countries and selling over 8 million copies worldwide, promotion was cut short amid Jackson's public dispute with Sony Music, leaving it without a supporting tour.

REMIX AND POSTHUMOUS STUDIO
11 / 1997

BLOOD ON THE DANCE FLOOR

OVERVIEW
Released on May 20, 1997, via Epic Records, Blood on the Dance Floor was a hybrid album pairing eight remixes of HIStory tracks with five brand-new original songs recorded with Teddy Riley and Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis.

SOUND & THEMES
Blending club-ready electronic dance music, house, industrial funk, and new jack swing, the new tracks explore dark, gothic themes of betrayal, addiction, paranoia, and media persecution.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Blood on the Dance Floor," "Ghosts," "Is It Scary," "Morphine," and "Superfly Sister". Reaching No. 1 in the UK and spawning the accompanying short film Ghosts, it sold over 6 million copies worldwide to become the best-selling remix album of all time.

12 / 2010

MICHAEL

OVERVIEW
Released on December 10, 2010, via Epic Records, Michael was Jackson's first posthumous studio album, compiling previously unreleased vault tracks completed by producers including Teddy Riley, Tricky Stewart, and Akon.

SOUND & THEMES
Mixing contemporary 2010s pop, R&B, and rock arrangements around Jackson's archival vocals, it spans uplifting anthems of unity, romantic devotion, and reflections on fame and legacy.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Hold My Hand" (with Akon), "Hollywood Tonight," "Behind the Mask," "Best of Joy," and "(I Like) The Way You Love Me". It debuted at No. 3 on the Billboard 200 and reached No. 1 in several countries, though its release was marked by fan debate over vocal authenticity on three tracks later removed from streaming in 2022.

13 / 2014

XSCAPE

OVERVIEW
Released on May 13, 2014, via Epic Records, Xscape was Jackson's second posthumous studio album, featuring eight unreleased songs recorded between 1983 and 1999 and "contemporized" by executive producer Timbaland alongside Rodney Jerkins and Stargate.

SOUND & THEMES
Pairing Jackson's original vocal performances with sleek, modern R&B, trap-influenced pop, and retro-disco production, it touches on escapism, romance, heartbreak, and social commentary.

KEY TRACKS & IMPACT
Features "Love Never Felt So Good" (with Justin Timberlake), "A Place with No Name," "Slave to the Rhythm," "Chicago," and "Xscape". Widely praised for including both modernized mixes and Jackson's untouched original demos on the deluxe edition, it debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200 and gave him a top-10 Hot 100 hit ("Love Never Felt So Good") across five consecutive decades.
