ON STAGE & ON SCREEN

LIVE PERFORMANCES

The performance notes below are organized by song and use the event details supplied for this album.

GOT TO BE THERE

LIVE AT THE FORUM • 26 AUGUST 1972

Released officially on Live at the Forum, featuring tight vocal harmonies with his brothers and punchy brass backing.

GOT TO BE THERE

HELLZAPOPPIN' TV SPECIAL • 1 MARCH 1972

His first televised performance of the song, famous for the theatrical garage-door opening reveal.

GOT TO BE THERE

LIVE IN JAPAN • 30 APRIL 1973

An extended, nearly four-minute rendition captured in Osaka with a spoken introduction.

GOT TO BE THERE

THE SONNY & CHER COMEDY HOUR • 1972

A crisp, charismatic television showcase backed by a live studio orchestra.

GOT TO BE THERE

SOUL TRAIN • OCTOBER 1972

A fan-favorite TV appearance where young Michael delivers effortless high notes in front of the studio dancers.

AIN'T NO SUNSHINE

LIVE AT THE FORUM • 26 AUGUST 1972

Widely considered his definitive live take, opening with a soulful spoken-word monologue before building into emotional ad-libs.

AIN'T NO SUNSHINE

THE FLIP WILSON SHOW • NOVEMBER 1972

A raw, dramatic television vocal performance that highlights his mature phrasing at age 14.

AIN'T NO SUNSHINE

LIVE IN JAPAN • APRIL 1973

Features an extended instrumental breakdown and passionate vocal runs.

AIN'T NO SUNSHINE

THE JACKSONS VARIETY SHOW • 1976

A later teen-era performance showing his vocal transition into a richer baritone/tenor range.

I WANNA BE WHERE YOU ARE

LIVE AT THE FORUM • 26 AUGUST 1972

Celebrated for its high-energy choreography and soaring vocal control.

I WANNA BE WHERE YOU ARE

LIVE IN JAPAN • APRIL 1973

Delivered at a brisk concert tempo with enthusiastic crowd interaction.

I WANNA BE WHERE YOU ARE

1973–1974 JACKSON 5 WORLD TOUR • VARIOUS BROADCASTS

Broadcasts including Dakar and Mexico showcase his fluid footwork alongside his brothers' synchronized backing vocals.

ROCKIN' ROBIN

AMERICAN BANDSTAND • OCTOBER 1972

A lively solo TV spot introduced by Dick Clark.

ROCKIN' ROBIN

LIVE IN MEXICO • 1975

An energetic mid-1970s stadium rendition with a fuller funk-rock band arrangement.

ROCKIN' ROBIN

OFF THE WALL MEDLEY • TRIUMPH TOUR • 1981

Adult Michael revived the song as part of a high-speed nostalgia medley, giving it a slick, disco-funk rhythm.

ROCKIN' ROBIN

1972–1973 JACKSON 5 CONCERTS

Performed as a fast-paced crowd-pleaser complete with bird-call whistles and spins.

YOU'VE GOT A FRIEND

LIVE AT THE FORUM • 26 AUGUST 1972

The only widely circulating official live recording of this track, featuring a tender, slow-building vocal duet dynamic with Jermaine Jackson.

STUDIO TRACKS

RARELY OR NEVER PERFORMED LIVE

The remaining five album tracks—Girl Don't Take Your Love from Me, In Our Small Way, Wings of My Love, Maria (You Were the Only One), and Love Is Here and Now You're Gone—were studio recordings that were not part of Michael Jackson's or the Jackson 5's regular live setlists.
