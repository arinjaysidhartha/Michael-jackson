MICHAEL JACKSON • 1972 • MOTOWN

GOT TO BE THERE

Michael Jackson's debut solo studio album, released on January 24, 1972. Recorded while he was still performing with the Jackson 5, the album brings together Motown soul, pop, and tender ballads.

OTHER APPEARANCES

These appearances were included in your notes. I have kept them here, but have not attached a player because I could not verify a YouTube upload of that exact song at that exact event:

  • Got to Be There: Sonny & Cher Comedy Hour (1972); Soul Train (1972); Live in Japan, Osaka (30 April 1973).
  • Ain't No Sunshine: The Flip Wilson Show (1972); Live in Japan (April 1973); The Jacksons Variety Show (1976). The exact song/event matches need checking; archive listings show a Jackson 5 Flip Wilson appearance on 26 October 1972 but do not confirm this song.
  • I Wanna Be Where You Are: 1973–1974 Jackson 5 tour dates, including the Mexico and Dakar appearances mentioned in your notes.
  • Rockin' Robin: Jackson 5 concerts (1972–1973); Live in Mexico (1975); the Triumph Tour (1981) medley mention.

Date correction: Michael's American Bandstand solo appearance featuring “Rockin' Robin” is documented as 1 July 1972, not October 1972.
