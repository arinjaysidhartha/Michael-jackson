LIVE PERFORMANCE ARCHIVE

This archive separates confirmed song performances from studio tracks with no reliable live record located. A song that was performed on a tour may have many show dates; the linked archive pages list documented shows individually. “Top 10” below means ten documented examples where the available source supports them—not ten invented video uploads. Some songs were never performed live, and some surviving records are audio-only or television appearances.

IMPORTANT

NO FALSE PERFORMANCE CLAIMS

Not every studio-album song has a known live performance. In particular, several Motown album cuts, many Invincible tracks, and the posthumous album tracks have no verified public live performance by Michael Jackson. Those songs should be labelled “no verified live performance located,” not padded with unrelated concert clips. A tour setlist entry proves that the song was on the programme; it does not prove that a surviving video upload exists.
